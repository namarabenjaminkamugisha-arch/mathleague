MathLeague

Privacy

MathLeague does not collect your personal information. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server storing anything about you.

What is stored, and where

Your score, streak, league and topic results are saved on your own device, in your browser's local storage. They never leave it. Nobody else — including us — can see them.

Clearing your browser data, or using Reset progress in the app, erases all of it permanently. There is no copy anywhere else.

Adverts

MathLeague shows no adverts unless you ask for one. There are no banners and nothing that interrupts you.

If you choose to watch a short advert to earn a free power-up, that advert is supplied by Google. Only then is any advertising code loaded at all. We request non-personalised adverts only, which means they are not based on any profile of you. Google may still process limited technical information, such as your approximate country and device type, in order to deliver the advert. Google's own policy explains this at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

You never have to watch an advert. Declining costs you nothing and the app works exactly the same.

Children

MathLeague is intended for students, including children. We request non-personalised adverts for everyone, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone of any age.

What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • No cookies set by MathLeague itself.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because none is collected.
  • No email address, phone number, name or location is ever asked for.

Working offline

The app stores its own files on your device so it keeps working without internet. Those are program files, not information about you.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example if accounts are added — this page will be updated before the change reaches you.

Questions about this page can be raised at the project's issue tracker.
